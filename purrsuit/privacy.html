Purrsuit 🐾 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

The short version: everything stays on your device, and we collect nothing. 💗

Purrsuit ("the app") is a goal-companion app published by HApps. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data collection

Purrsuit does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app:

  • has no user accounts and never asks you to sign in;
  • contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind;
  • makes no network connections; it works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (your goals, sessions, streaks, diary entries, little plans, your name, and your cat's name) is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it (the app deliberately opts out of Android's cloud auto-backup so nothing is silently kept).

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings. That file is created only when you tap the button, and you choose where it goes. The app never sends it anywhere on its own.

Sharing

The "show off your cat" and recap cards create an image on your device and hand it to Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app and send it yourself.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone. You can turn them off any time in Settings, or decline the permission; the app works fine without them.

Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show the reminders you set
Exact alarmsSo reminders arrive at the exact minute you chose (optional; the app falls back to approximate timing if declined)
Boot / vibrate / wake lockSo reminders survive a phone restart and can gently vibrate

Children

Purrsuit does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase everything inside the app, or just uninstall it. Either removes all data, because it only ever existed on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date changed before that version ships.
