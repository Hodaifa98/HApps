Purrsuit 🐾

A tiny cheer-cat for your big dreams.

Purrsuit is a goal companion for Android. You set the goals that matter to you, show up for short focused sessions, and a little cat keeps you company: cheering, napping, wearing the outfits you unlock, and living in a room you decorate as you go.

What's inside

  • Goals with a finish line, streaks, and forgiving cat-nap tokens for the days you miss
  • Focus sessions, including 3-minute tiny ones for the days that feel impossible
  • A journal, little plans, and a water tracker
  • A closet of accessories and a decorable room your cat actually lives in
  • Shareable monthly recap cards and a trophy shelf
  • English, Français, Deutsch, Español

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no network connections at all. Everything you write stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
